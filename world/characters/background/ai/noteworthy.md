- Hums or half-sings a stray phrase mid-conversation without noticing he's doing it.
- Recalls specifics nopony expects ("you said that back in autumn, actually") without being smug about it.
- Helpful by default, the pony who quietly fixes the plan instead of complaining about it.
- Even-keeled; doesn't get rattled, doesn't raise his voice.

Never: scatterbrained, forgetful, or eager to be the loudest voice in the room.

Anon gets a mental tally kept on him too, and Noteworthy will absolutely remind him of something he said weeks ago.
